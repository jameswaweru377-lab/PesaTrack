# Salary receipts and spending periods

Home always links to Salary & payday, including when no cycle exists. Save the employer/source and receiving account, then confirm an existing incoming transaction or enter an actually received salary with amount and receipt date. Recent source receipts cover the previous three months. Older manual dates are also supported. Confirming an existing receipt never inserts another transaction. Manual confirmation explicitly starts a cycle, including early or irregular paydays.

Activity offers Calendar month and Pay cycle. Both use TransactionStore's trusted accounting, refund, reversal, duplicate, fee and transfer rules. Pay cycles start inclusively at the recorded receipt time and end exclusively at the next actual salary receipt. Date-only manual entries start at local midnight. All captured spending in the period is counted across accounts; this does not claim to trace individual funds back to salary or savings. The current cycle stays open while salary is late. Previous-cycle comparison shows amounts and explains that the current cycle is incomplete.

Salary reminders are opt-in. Android notification permission is requested when the switch is enabled. A local WorkManager check runs every 12 hours and once after settings changes; Android may delay background work. After the full configured payday window ends, it asks whether salary arrived. Add salary opens the salary screen in cold and warm app launches. Not yet received defers another check for three days. Manual and automatic receipts suppress future missing-receipt checks for that period. Reminders can be switched off. No salary details are uploaded or shown on the lock screen.

A late coded message can attach to a manual salary only if one receipt uniquely matches the salary rule, receiving account, amount and local date. The user's receipt date, classification and transaction identity remain intact. The code is saved for replay deduplication. Different dates/accounts/amounts and ambiguous receipts are not automatically combined.

Validation covers expected windows in Nairobi, first-cycle absence, cross-month windows, February, early salary, old history, late SMS replay, separate accounts/dates, pay-cycle boundaries, all-account spending and first-cycle UI entry points. Physical-device notification timing and OEM battery restrictions require device verification.
