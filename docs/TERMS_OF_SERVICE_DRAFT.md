## 1. Service and eligibility
PesaTrack is a personal-finance organisation app that helps users review and organise supported messages, imported statements, manual entries, budgets, savings, bills, debts and financial-health context. Only use it on a device and with financial records you are authorised to access. A guardian should supervise any legally permitted use by minors, subject to applicable age and consent requirements.

## 2. Not a bank or financial adviser
The app does not hold deposits, initiate or execute transfers, issue loans, provide debt collection services, guarantee returns, establish credit scores or deliver regulated banking, investment, insurance, tax, accounting or legal advice. It is not affiliated with M-Pesa, Safaricom or any bank merely because it supports their transaction formats. Check the original provider's terms and official records.

## 3. Accuracy and limitations
Imported SMS, notifications, PDF statements and user-entered records may be incomplete, delayed, duplicated, wrongly attributed, miscategorised or based on partial account history. Balances, budgets, forecasts and financial-health assessments are estimates, not live or authoritative statements. Always verify transactions, beneficiaries, payments and balances with the provider before acting.

## 4. Personal lending records
A payment to someone does not automatically constitute a loan, debt or legally enforceable repayment obligation. The user must review and explicitly confirm the payment's purpose, beneficiary, amount and evidence before marking it as a loan or repayment. Records within PesaTrack alone are not proof of a legally recoverable debt. Preserve original statements and separate loan agreements.

## 5. User responsibilities
Protect your device, app-lock PIN and backup passphrase; supply accurate details; review and correct imported records; use third-party names, contact details and financial information only where lawful; and promptly report security or data issues through the private contact provided at launch. Do not use the app to harass, intimidate or unlawfully process another person's data.

## 6. Privacy, consent and permissions
The separate Privacy Policy explains data handled locally, optional SMS and notification permissions, optional voice features, online merchant enrichment, user-controlled exports and backup practices. Android permissions can be refused or revoked. Disabling optional features may limit their functionality.

## 7. Data retention, exports, and backups
Local app data remains on your device subject to your deletion choices and Android storage behaviour. You control exports and encrypted backups stored outside the app. Deleted app data will not automatically remove exported files or backups. A forgotten backup passphrase may mean that exported encrypted data cannot be recovered.

## 8. Availability and changes
The app may be updated, paused or modified for technical, security or compliance reasons, including changes in mobile-money or banking message formats and device permissions. Any material policy or service changes must be notified as required by law.

## 9. Fair limits on liability
To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the provider does not warrant that app-generated estimates, interpretations and forecasts will always be accurate or uninterrupted. Users are responsible for independently verifying critical financial decisions. **Nothing in this clause excludes or limits legal rights or liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded**, including liability for fraud, intentional misconduct, and mandatory Kenyan consumer or data-protection protections.

## 10. Complaints and disputes
Users must be given an accessible contact route for privacy, financial-data errors, consumer complaints, and suspected security incidents before release. Applicable Kenyan law governs disputes subject to any overriding mandatory rights. No compulsory arbitration or unfair waiver is imposed in this draft.

## 11. Updates and publisher identity
Before public publication, fill the verified operator's legal name, address, effective date, private support/privacy contact, version history and a public Privacy Policy URL, then obtain Kenyan legal review. Do not display this as an accepted legal agreement until those steps and the consent/versioning flow are complete.
