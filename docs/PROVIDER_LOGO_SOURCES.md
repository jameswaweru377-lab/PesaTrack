# Provider marks bundled in the Android app

PesaTrack keeps provider marks on-device; **no logos are fetched at runtime**.
These marks identify third-party institutions, not endorsement, partnership,
authentication, or bank connectivity. Provider names and logos are trademarks
of their respective owners.

| Bundled resource | Source | Reuse information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pesatrack_provider_mpesa.png` | [M-PESA LOGO-01.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:M-PESA_LOGO-01.svg), attributed there to Vodafone | Wikimedia Commons describes the simple-shape/text logo as public domain under copyright. Trademark caution applies. Rasterized to PNG. |
| `pesatrack_provider_equity.png` | [Equity Group Logo.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Equity_Group_Logo.png), attributed there to Equity Bank | Wikimedia Commons marks the logo public domain as simple geometry/text and documents permission correspondence. Trademark caution applies. Original PNG. |
| `pesatrack_provider_absa.png` | [ABSA Group Limited Logo.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:ABSA_Group_Limited_Logo.svg), attributed there to Absa Group Limited | Wikimedia Commons describes the text/geometry logo as public domain under copyright. Trademark caution applies. Rasterized to PNG. |
| `pesatrack_provider_im.webp` | [Imbank-logo.webp](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Imbank-logo.webp), attributed there to I&M Bank | Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International. Original unmodified WebP. License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ |

For other institutions, PesaTrack displays a locally drawn initials/symbol
tile until a suitable, verifiable image and its reuse terms are available.
This deliberately avoids unlicensed artwork and runtime calls to logo CDNs.
