# PesaTrack Code of Conduct

PesaTrack welcomes thoughtful feedback and respectful collaboration. Our community discussions should be useful, inclusive, and safe, especially because the project deals with personal-finance information.

## Our expectations

When participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, or other project spaces:

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Disagreement about a feature or implementation is welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Give clear, actionable feedback and assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and ways of communicating.
- Protect privacy. Use invented examples; never post someone's real financial messages, account details, phone numbers, credentials, or private screenshots.
- Respect others' work and intellectual-property rights.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, hate speech, discriminatory insults, sexualized harassment, deliberate intimidation, unwanted disclosure of personal information, spam, and repeated hostile or disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy covers project-maintained GitHub spaces and interactions representing the PesaTrack project. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, request a change in behaviour, restrict participation, or block accounts as appropriate to protect the community.

For ordinary moderation questions, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub. **For a sensitive conduct concern, do not post identifying details publicly.** Use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile; if none is available, open a minimal issue asking for a private reporting route without including the allegation or personal details.

Reports will be handled with discretion as far as reasonably possible. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed on third-party platforms. A person directly involved in a report should not be its sole decision-maker where another appropriate reviewer is available.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate, private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
